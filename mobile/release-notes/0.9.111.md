### Features
- Tapping a PDF in chat opens it in the phone's own viewer

### Improvements
- A message that is still sending when the desktop restarts the tab's terminal now follows the tab to its new terminal, when Claude is running there
- The line that says why something could not open or send has its own readable surface instead of being drawn over the chat, and no longer blocks swipes on the chat

### Security & Bug Fixes
- A message is never typed into a shell: when Claude's input box is not on the desktop screen, the send stops and keeps your draft
- Switching tabs while a message is sending no longer sends it to the other tab
- A sheet no longer leaves an invisible layer that blocks every tap after it closes, including after pressing Back while it was closing
- Sheets close once and smoothly: no double animation, no jump before closing, no sudden drop with the keyboard up, and the side drawer no longer snaps back under your finger
