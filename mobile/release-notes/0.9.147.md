### Features
- Sign in to more than one Anthropic account: Voice settings lists each account with its own Sign out, and "Add another account". Sessions from either account show their model and pictures
- Clear saved sign-ins, if the phone's saved sign-ins ever cannot be read

### Improvements
- When Claude switches a session to a fallback model, the model sheet keeps "Opus 5.5" on the Opus row and checks the model actually running under More models

### Security & Bug Fixes
- A chat no longer shows the terminal's navy background behind its messages
- One refused token refresh no longer signs an account out for good
- The session match refuses to guess when a candidate session cannot be read
