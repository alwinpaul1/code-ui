### Features
- The Background tasks sheet now matches the Claude app: an agent shows its description (or its current step while a tool runs), its time counts from its last wake, shells have a terminal icon, and every running task on a terminal tab gets a Stop
- Reasoning effort is read from the chat's own history, so it shows straight away when you open an older chat

### Improvements
- Finished shells leave the Running list as soon as Claude's footer shows none left
- Quoted blocks keep their formatting, links and line breaks, and copying a reply keeps its formatting

### Security & Bug Fixes
- A pasted screenshot leaves the composer together with your words when you send
- The "Sending…" label under a sent message is gone
- A text message whose send times out while you're in another chat is sent once when you return, if it didn't arrive
- A photo message is never sent twice, and after a restart its words return to the box
- The connection log no longer fills up with send timings
