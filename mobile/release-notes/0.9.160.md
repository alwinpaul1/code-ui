### Features
- Continue with Google works in the in-app sign-in: Google opens inside the sign-in screen, as Orca's own browser does it, instead of a blank Chrome tab
- Dictation in a Claude session always uses the Anthropic account that owns the session. When the phone has not matched it yet, it asks Anthropic at the tap, and it never falls back to another account

### Improvements
- Dictation hears you from the tap: the mic opens at once and nothing said while the account is looked up or the connection opens is lost
- The sign-in page loads behind Clawd dancing, and claude.ai no longer runs its security check again on every sign-in
- The sign-in page and the Anthropic accounts list drop their small explanatory lines

### Security & Bug Fixes
- A dictation stopped or cancelled while the mic was still opening is stopped or cancelled, not left recording
- A take stopped before the voice connection opens still waits for its transcript, and one whose connection never opens ends with an error after 10 s
- Dictation says plainly when a session belongs to an account that is not signed in on the phone, and only after every signed-in account was searched in full
