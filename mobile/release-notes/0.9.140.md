### Features
- Tapping the mic turns the composer into a recording row like the Claude app's: Cancel, a scrolling waveform that follows your voice, Stop, and Send
- Pictures Claude reads from your project, or names in its reply, show inline as thumbnails that open full screen

### Improvements
- The composer matches the Claude app's spacing, text size, placeholder, buttons and colours
- Bottom sheets have the Claude app's corners, colours, handle and backdrop, with the same motion as before
- The host menu shows the Mac or PC controls at once on the first open, at a steady height
- Dictated text no longer gets a space before punctuation

### Security & Bug Fixes
- Voice dictation signs in with the same scopes as Claude Code; sign in once more from Voice settings when asked
- A voice server error now shows its reason and request id
- Coming back to the app from the Home screen no longer flashes the terminal at desktop width
- Scrolling no longer ends a text selection in the chat, and the terminal keeps its selection on a scroll
