### Improvements
- The scroll-to-latest button looks, sits and moves like the Claude app's
- The copy icon under a reply is drawn the way the Claude app draws it
- A background task's notification shows as a short status line, not your message full of XML

### Security & Bug Fixes
- Scrolling no longer starts a new text selection while you hold one, so the words you are copying stay selected
- Scrolling over a queued message no longer selects its words
- Copying a reply longer than 100,000 characters copies the first 100,000, as the Claude app does
