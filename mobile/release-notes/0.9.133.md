### Features
- The chat screen and message box use the Claude app's colours in light and dark mode

### Improvements
- Opening a terminal tab goes straight from the empty screen to its output at phone size, in phone mode, with no blank flash or desktop-mode bar first
- Pinch, pan and double-tap zoom in the picture viewer keep it open; it closes on ✕, Back or a swipe down, and its background is solid black
- The Files tile in the + sheet uses a file-upload icon, like the Claude app

### Security & Bug Fixes
- Tapping a notification opens the exact tab that sent it, whether the app was closed, in the background or on another tab, and says so when that tab has since closed
